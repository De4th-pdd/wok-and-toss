## 1. Executive Summary & Asset Provenance

An exhaustive audit of the supplied and local asset repositories was performed across all storage locations. While the external Google Drive directory (`https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1JYGWtwUVITPxmLpjH0LBqlrplTxfomSF`) gates unauthenticated automated scrapers via Google Drive sign-in, the complete asset collection was discovered in local development archives on system storage:

## 4. Duplicate Detection & Asset Cleanliness

During the audit, exact duplicate files and variant suffix instances were identified:

1. **`WhatsApp Video 2026-10-16 at 5.57.05 PM (1).mp4` vs `WhatsApp Video 2026-10-16 at 5.57.05 PM.mp4`:**
   - Byte comparison confirms identical stream content (Sichuan Kung Pao chicken wok toss).
   - *Action:* The un-suffixed file is designated as canonical; `(1)` is discarded from production bundles.
2. **`WhatsApp Video 2026-10-16 at 2.29.07 PM (1).mp4`, `(2).mp4`, `(3).mp4`:**
   - These are separate video segments recorded within the same 60-second burst:
     - `(1)`: Pan over table setup.
     - `(2)`: Close-up on soup bowl.
     - `(3)`: High-quality ladle scoop into Hot & Sour Soup.
   - *Action:* Kept `(3)` as the primary soup footage; others cataloged as secondary b-roll.
3. **`WhatsApp Video 2026-10-16 at 2.29.08 PM (1).mp4`, `(2).mp4`, `(3).mp4`:**
   - `(1)`: Side angle of noodle box.
   - `(2)`: Sharp fork lift of Chilli Dry Chicken (selected as canonical for chilli sizzle).
   - `(3)`: 3-compartment combo lunch box (selected as canonical for combo presentation).
